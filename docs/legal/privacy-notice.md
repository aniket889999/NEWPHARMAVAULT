## What we collect and why
| Data | Purpose | Basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Account: name, email, role, sign-in security data | Running your account, security | Consent / legitimate use [TO CONFIRM] |
| Prescriptions, pharmacy invoices, claim forms you upload, and details read from them (OCR) | Checking that a prescription and an invoice match, so a pharmacist can verify the case | Consent given when you upload |
| Claim details (insurer, policy number, amount) | Sending a verified case to the insurer you choose | Separate, specific consent per claim |
| Activity and audit records | Security, accountability, dispute handling | Legal obligation / legitimate use [TO CONFIRM] |

Your documents are processed on our servers. Private case data is **not** sent to web search or to any external AI service.

## Who sees your data
- The pharmacy organization handling your case and its pharmacists.
- An insurer, **only** for a case you submit to it, **only** while your consent is active. You can withdraw consent by withdrawing the claim.
- Platform administrators, for support and legal requests, under audit.

## How long we keep it
- Document files and details read from them: removed [TO CONFIRM: 365] days after a case is finished and no claim is open (`RETENTION_CASE_DAYS`).
- Notifications: 180 days.
- Claim decisions, review decisions and audit records: kept as the accountability record for [TO CONFIRM: period required by insurer/regulator rules]. They do not contain your documents.

## Your rights
- **Access / copy:** Account Security → *Download My Data* (instant).
- **Correction:** correct extracted fields in your case before review; contact the pharmacy for anything else.
- **Erasure:** Account Security → *Request Deletion*. Completed within 30 days. Open claims must be withdrawn or decided first. Records listed above as accountability records are kept.
- **Withdraw consent:** withdraw a claim; the insurer's access ends immediately.
- **Grievance:** see `grievance-redressal.md`. You may also approach the Data Protection Board of India.

## Security
Encrypted connections; documents encrypted at rest (AES-256-GCM) when enabled; two-factor authentication for staff; tamper-evident audit log.
