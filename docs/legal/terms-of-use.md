# Terms of Use — DRAFT (needs legal review)

1. **What PharmaVault is.** A tool that helps a pharmacist compare a prescription with a pharmacy invoice and helps a claimant send a verified case to an insurer. It is a research prototype [TO CONFIRM: commercial status].
2. **No medical advice.** PharmaVault does not diagnose, prescribe or substitute medicines. The Evidence Chat explains the evidence in a case with citations; it is not medical advice.
3. **Humans decide.** Pharmacists decide whether a case is verified. Insurers decide claims. Risk signals are prompts for review, not proof of fraud.
4. **Your uploads.** Upload only documents you are entitled to share. Do not upload other people's documents without their permission.
5. **Staff accounts.** Pharmacy and insurer staff must keep two-factor authentication on, must not share accounts, and must record a reason for every decision. Organization administrators must confirm a pharmacist's registration before they verify cases.
6. **Settlement.** Payment settlement shown in the product is simulated unless your insurer states otherwise. [TO CONFIRM]
